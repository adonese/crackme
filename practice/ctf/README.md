# CTF Practice

CTF bundles live here. These are less uniform than classic crackmes, but they
are good practice because they include handouts, source, and official solutions.

Current bundle:

- `crackmes-one-ctf-2026/` - public crackmes.one RE CTF 2026 archive.

Spoiler habit:

1. Open `handout/` or `Handout/` first.
2. Ignore `solution`, `Solution`, source folders, and top-level challenge
   READMEs until you have made a first attempt.
3. Use those files as guidance after you get stuck.
